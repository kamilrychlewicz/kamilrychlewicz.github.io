Research

My work is primarily focused on different aspects of geometry, and I work on the intersection of algebraic geometry, complex geometry and algebraic topology. The topics I research include:

  • equivariant cohomology theories;
  • topological invariants of algebraic varieties;
  • characteristic classes of singular spaces;
  • combinatorics of flag and Schubert varieties;
  • toric varieties and related commutative algebra.

Apart from the work I publish, I also enjoy thinking about homotopy theory, model structures, infinity-categories, intersection theory and moduli spaces.
