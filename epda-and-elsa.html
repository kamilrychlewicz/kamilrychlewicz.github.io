EPDA and ELSA

I am currently the treasurer of EPDA, the EPFL postdoc association. I am also appointed to the Mental Health Council at EPFL as the representative of ELSA, the association of postdocs, PhD students and scientists. Feel free to message me if you are a postdoc, PhD student or scientist, and

  • would like to organise an event on campus for the community, but do not know how to start and where to get money;
  • do not understand some policies at EPFL and need clarification;
  • would like to express your opinion about something, or suggest some improvement, but do not know where to address it;
  • you have experience or opinions you would like to share, concerning life on campus and mental health.
